# Memoir of a Murderer

---

## Overview
**Memoir of a Murderer** is a *psychological thriller* centered around a former serial killer diagnosed with Alzheimer's disease who fights to protect his daughter from a new killer.

> "Do not trust your own memories."

### Key Themes
1. Memory Loss & Identity
2. Paternal Protection
3. Moral Ambiguity

### Quick Facts
- **Genre**: Crime/Psychological Thriller/Mystery
- **Primary Language**: Korean
- **Tag**: `Thriller`
